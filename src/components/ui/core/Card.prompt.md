The default content container — case studies, feature tiles, form panels.

```jsx
<Card tone="default" interactive>
  <h3>Legal AI Agent</h3>
  <p>
    AI to generate contracts, adhere to policies, and manage conflict of
    interest.
  </p>
</Card>
```

Tones: `default`, `subtle`, `accent` (purple-tinted), `inverse` (on dark sections). Use `GlassPanel` instead when the card sits over photography or a reflection background.
